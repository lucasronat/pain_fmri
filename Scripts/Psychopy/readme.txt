Here you will find the Psychopy scripts for the various stages of our protocol on the effect of expectations on pain perception: 

1) Calibration phase

Determines the temperatures corresponding to mild pain (30/100 on a VAS scale), moderate pain (55/100), and severe pain (80/100) for each participant.

2) Verification Phase

Confirms the ability to distinguish between the three selected temperatures and their associations with low, moderate, and high pain estimates.

3) Learning Phase

Familiarizes participants with the task and the congruent associations between visual cues (low, moderate, and high) and thermal stimuli (low, moderate, and high).

4) Expectation Effect Phase in fMRI

Based on the learning task, consisting of 3 runs of 40 trials each, repeating each experimental condition 8 times (24 congruent trials and 16 incongruent trials).
